Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Last updated October 30, 2026

Who we are

Grow.Media ("we", "us") is an independent online publication at grow.media. You can reach us through our contact page.

Information we collect

Information you give us: when you submit a form (newsletter, growth plan, contact, contest, careers, support pledge or partnership), we collect the details you enter, such as your name, email, website and message.

Automatically collected information: like most websites, our hosting provider and third-party services may log technical data such as IP address, browser type, pages visited and referring site.

Tool inputs: our calculators run entirely in your browser. The numbers you type into tools are not sent to us or stored by us.

How we use information

We use information to respond to requests, send newsletters you subscribed to, deliver growth plans, run contests, process applications and pledges, improve the site, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

Advertising and Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info and, in the EU/UK, youronlinechoices.eu.

For more on how Google uses data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), we request consent before personalized ads are shown.

Third-party services

Form processing: form submissions are delivered to our private inbox by a third-party form-processing service (FormSubmit), which handles data under its own privacy policy.

Embedded videos: YouTube videos are embedded in privacy-enhanced mode and only load after you click play; YouTube may then set cookies under Google's policies.

Fonts and hosting: we use Google Fonts and host the site on GitHub Pages, which may process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver content.

Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember preferences such as dark mode and whether you've dismissed the cookie notice. Advertising partners use cookies as described above. See our Cookie Policy.

Data retention

We keep form submissions only as long as needed for the purpose they were submitted for, or as required by law. Newsletter subscribers can unsubscribe at any time.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, Canada's PIPEDA and Québec's Law 25), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal information, withdraw consent, and object to certain processing. To make a request, choose "Privacy request" on our contact page. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

Children

Grow.Media is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards.

Changes

We may update this policy. The "last updated" date shows when it last changed.
