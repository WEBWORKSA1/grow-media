Follower Growth Calculator

Audience growth compounds, so small differences in monthly growth rate create large differences a year out. Use this to set realistic goals and to see what growth rate a target actually demands.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is stored or sent.

Results appear here as you type.

How the Follower Growth Calculator works

MetricFormula
Projectionfollowers × (1 + monthly growth rate)^months
Months to goallog(goal ÷ followers) ÷ log(1 + growth rate)
Required rate(goal ÷ followers)^(1 ÷ months) − 1

Track growth as a monthly percentage, not raw followers: going from 1,000 to 1,100 is the same 10% effort as going from 10,000 to 11,000. If the required rate looks unrealistic, lengthen the timeline or add a growth lever such as collaborations, a new format, or cross-posting to a second platform.

FAQ

Is buying followers ever worth it?

No. Fake followers lower engagement rate, hurt distribution and are visible to brands. Grow with content and collaborations instead.

Why has my growth slowed?

Growth slows when content stops reaching new audiences. Refresh formats, collaborate, and study which recent posts brought in new followers.

What growth rate should I target?

Start from your trailing three-month average and aim to improve it by a few points through specific experiments.
