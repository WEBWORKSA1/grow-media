Engagement Rate Calculator

Engagement rate tells you what share of your audience actually interacts with a post. It is a better health signal than follower count, because it shows whether people care about what you publish, and it is one of the first numbers brands check before offering a sponsorship.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is stored or sent.

Results appear here as you type.

How the Engagement Rate Calculator works

MetricFormula
Engagement rate by followers(likes + comments + shares + saves) ÷ followers × 100
Engagement rate by reach(likes + comments + shares + saves) ÷ reach × 100

Use follower-based engagement to compare yourself with other accounts, since follower counts are public. Use reach-based engagement to judge your own content, because it only counts people who actually saw the post. Calculate it across your last 10–12 posts rather than one, so a single outlier doesn't distort the picture. Engagement naturally falls as accounts grow, which is why the ranges below are grouped by audience size.

Reference ranges

ScenarioValue
Under 10K followers~3–6%
10K–100K~1.5–3.5%
100K–1M~1–2.5%
1M+~0.5–1.8%

Commonly cited planning ranges; they vary by platform, niche and format. Use them as a starting point, not a verdict.

FAQ

What is a good engagement rate?

It depends on audience size and platform. Smaller accounts usually see higher rates. If you sit within or above the typical range for your size, your content is resonating.

Should saves and shares count?

Yes. Saves and shares signal stronger intent than likes, and many platforms weight them heavily in distribution, so include them whenever you can see them.

Why did my engagement drop as I grew?

New followers are less familiar with you than early fans, and distribution widens to colder audiences. A gradual decline with growth is normal; a sudden drop usually points to a content or format change.
