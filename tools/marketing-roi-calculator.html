Marketing ROI Calculator

Revenue-based ROI flatters campaigns because it ignores what the product costs to deliver. This calculator measures return on the gross profit a campaign generated, which is the number that actually pays for marketing.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is stored or sent.

Results appear here as you type.

How the Marketing ROI Calculator works

MetricFormula
Gross profitrevenue × gross margin
ROI(gross profit − cost) ÷ cost × 100
Break-even revenuecost ÷ gross margin

Include every cost: ad spend, tools, freelancers, content production and your own time if you can value it. Attribute revenue consistently, using the same window and model for every campaign you compare. If ROI is negative but the campaign brings in customers who buy again, look at lifetime value with the LTV:CAC calculator before cutting it.

FAQ

Should I use revenue or profit for ROI?

Use gross profit whenever you know your margin. Revenue ROI is useful for quick comparisons but overstates real return.

How do I attribute revenue to a campaign?

Use tracked links (UTMs), unique promo codes or platform conversion tracking, and keep the attribution window the same across campaigns.

What about brand campaigns?

Brand campaigns pay back slowly and indirectly. Track leading indicators such as branded search, direct traffic and list growth alongside ROI.
