YouTube Partner Program Checker

Before ad revenue switches on, YouTube requires a minimum audience. This checker shows how close you are on each path so you can decide whether to push long-form watch time or Shorts views.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is stored or sent.

How the YouTube Partner Program Checker works

MetricFormula
Requirement1,000 subscribers AND (4,000 watch hours in 12 months OR 10M Shorts views in 90 days)
Progressthe lower of subscriber progress and your best watch-time path

There are two ways to qualify: long-form watch time or Shorts views. Most channels pick one path and optimize for it rather than splitting effort. Long-form watch hours accumulate faster if your videos are 8–20 minutes with strong retention; Shorts views accumulate faster if you can publish daily. YouTube also offers lower-tier fan-funding features at smaller thresholds in many regions, and every rule can change, so confirm the current requirements in YouTube Studio's Earn section.

FAQ

Do Shorts watch hours count toward 4,000 hours?

No. Shorts views count on their own path; long-form watch hours come from regular videos.

Do private or deleted videos count?

Only public watch time counts toward the threshold.

What happens after I qualify?

You apply in YouTube Studio, your channel is reviewed against policies, and once approved you can turn on ads and other monetization features.
