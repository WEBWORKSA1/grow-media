Funnel Conversion Calculator

Every business is a funnel: strangers become visitors, visitors become leads, leads become customers. This model shows how many customers your current rates produce and which stage has the most room to improve.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is stored or sent.

Results appear here as you type.

How the Funnel Conversion Calculator works

MetricFormula
Customersvisitors × r1 × r2 × r3
Revenuecustomers × average order value
Revenue per visitorrevenue ÷ visitors

Because the stages multiply, improving the weakest stage usually beats buying more traffic. Revenue per visitor is a useful ceiling for what you can afford to pay for a click. Revisit these rates monthly and keep a backlog of experiments for the leakiest stage.

Reference ranges

ScenarioValue
Website visitor → lead~1–5%
Lead → qualified~20–50%
Qualified → customer~10–30%

Very broad ranges; B2B, e-commerce and local services differ substantially.

FAQ

What is a good conversion rate?

There's no universal figure. Compare against your own history first, then against businesses with similar price points and traffic sources.

Where should I start optimizing?

Start with the stage that has the lowest rate relative to its typical range, and test one change at a time.

How does this relate to the AARRR funnel?

This tool covers acquisition and activation-to-revenue. Retention and referral add further multipliers on top.
