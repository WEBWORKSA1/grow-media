Sponsorship Rate Calculator

Most creators under-price their first sponsorships. This calculator builds a defensible rate from the inputs brands care about: expected views, how engaged your audience is, the size of the deliverable, and the extra value of usage rights and exclusivity.

Many creators quote roughly $15–$50 per 1,000 views; niche matters.

Brands buy outcomes, so anchor your price on expected views rather than follower count. A dedicated video carries more value than a 60-second mention; short-form and stories carry less. If a brand wants to run your content as paid ads (usage rights or whitelisting) or block you from working with competitors (exclusivity), price those separately with a clear time limit, often 30–90 days. When a brand pushes back on price, reduce scope rather than discounting the same work.

FAQ

How do I find my sponsor CPM?

Start with a conservative figure, track what brands accept, and raise it every few deals. Specialized, high-intent niches can command significantly higher CPMs than broad entertainment.

Should I charge for usage rights?

Yes. Usage rights let a brand use your content in their own advertising, which is extra value beyond your audience. Price them per month of usage.

What if I have high engagement but low views?

Lead with engagement, audience fit and conversion proof (affiliate clicks, promo-code uses). Consider a lower base fee plus a performance bonus.
