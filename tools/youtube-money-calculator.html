YouTube Money Calculator

This calculator turns your views into an ad-revenue estimate using RPM: the amount you actually earn per 1,000 views after the platform's share. It is the fastest way to sanity-check whether a channel idea, upload schedule or growth target is worth the effort.

Find your real RPM in YouTube Studio → Analytics → Revenue.

RPM differs from CPM. CPM is what advertisers pay per 1,000 ad impressions; RPM is what you receive per 1,000 total views, including views that showed no ad. RPM swings with your niche (finance and software tend to be higher than entertainment), viewer country, time of year (Q4 is usually strongest) and format. Shorts revenue comes from a shared pool and is typically a small fraction of long-form RPM. Ad revenue is rarely a creator's biggest income line, so treat this as one layer of your revenue stack.

FAQ

How much does YouTube pay per 1,000 views?

There is no fixed rate. Most long-form channels land somewhere between about $1 and $15 RPM, depending on niche, audience location and season.

Why is my RPM lower than my CPM?

CPM only counts monetized playbacks and is measured before the platform's share. RPM spreads your earnings across all views, including those without ads, so it is always lower.

Can I earn before joining the Partner Program?

Ad revenue requires the Partner Program. You can still earn earlier through sponsorships, affiliate links and your own products.
