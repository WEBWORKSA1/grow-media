Your list grows by the sign-ups you add minus the subscribers you lose. This projection makes that balance visible, including the point where your list stops growing unless you change traffic or conversion.

How the Email List Growth Calculator works

MetricFormula
Each monthlist + (visitors × opt-in rate) − (list × churn)
Long-run ceilingmonthly sign-ups ÷ monthly churn

The ceiling surprises most people: at a fixed number of sign-ups, a list eventually plateaus where new subscribers equal those leaving. Raising the ceiling means more traffic or a better opt-in rate, usually through a more specific lead magnet and better form placement. Lower churn helps too, but it's the slower lever.

FAQ

What is a good opt-in rate?

Sitewide forms often convert in the low single digits; dedicated landing pages with a strong offer can convert far higher.

How fast should my list grow?

Steady monthly growth beats spikes. Focus on a repeatable source of sign-ups such as search traffic, partnerships or a referral program.

Should I remove inactive subscribers?

Periodically, yes. Re-engage first, then remove long-term inactive contacts to protect deliverability.
