UTM Link Builder

UTM parameters are short tags added to a link that tell your analytics where a visitor came from. Without them, clicks from newsletters, bios, podcasts and partner posts often get grouped as 'direct' and your best channels become invisible.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is stored or sent.

Results appear here as you type.

How the UTM Link Builder works

MetricFormula
utm_sourcewhere the traffic comes from (newsletter, youtube, partner-name)
utm_mediumthe type of channel (email, social, cpc, referral)
utm_campaignthe specific push (spring-launch, black-friday)
utm_contentwhich link or creative (header-button, video-description)

Consistency is the whole game. Decide on a naming convention, write it down, and always use lowercase. This builder lowercases values and replaces spaces with underscores automatically. Don't add UTMs to internal links on your own site, because they overwrite the original source of the visit.

Reference ranges

ScenarioValue
Newsletter linksource=newsletter · medium=email
YouTube descriptionsource=youtube · medium=video
Instagram biosource=instagram · medium=social
Paid searchsource=google · medium=cpc

Example naming convention; consistency matters more than the exact words.

FAQ

Are UTM parameters bad for SEO?

No, as long as you use them on external links to your site and not on internal links. Canonical tags handle duplicate URLs.

Which parameters are required?

Source, medium and campaign are the essentials. Term and content are optional for finer detail.

Can I shorten UTM links?

Yes. Link shorteners keep the parameters when they redirect, which makes long links tidy for social posts.
