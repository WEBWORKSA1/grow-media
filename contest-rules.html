Official contest rules

Standard rules for Grow.Media growth contests. Season-specific dates, prizes and values are published in each season's announcement and on the contests page.

Last updated October 30, 2026

1. Sponsor

The contest is organized by Grow.Media (the "Sponsor"), an independent publication operating at grow.media. Contact the Sponsor through the contact page.

2. No purchase necessary

No purchase, payment or contribution is required to enter or win. Supporting Grow.Media does not improve your chances of winning.

3. Eligibility

Open to individuals who are at least 18 years old (or the age of majority in their jurisdiction) at the time of entry, where the contest is not prohibited by law. Employees and contractors of the Sponsor and their immediate households are not eligible. Void where prohibited or restricted by law.

4. Contest period

Exact start and end dates and the applicable time zone are announced by email to registrants and published on the contests page before each season opens. Entries received outside the contest period are not eligible.

5. How to enter

Register through the form on the contests page, complete the 30-day sprint, and submit before-and-after metrics with supporting screenshots and a written summary by the submission deadline. One entry per person per season. Automated, bulk or third-party entries are void.

6. Judging

Entries are judged by a panel appointed by the Sponsor on: growth rate relative to starting size (40%), quality and integrity of the growth, with no fake engagement, purchased followers or policy violations (30%), and the clarity and usefulness of the learning summary (30%). Judges' decisions are final. Ties are broken by the learning-summary score.

7. Prizes

Prizes for each season, including approximate retail value, are listed on the contests page and in the season announcement. Cash-equivalent growth grants are paid electronically. Prizes are non-transferable and may not be substituted except by the Sponsor, which may substitute a prize of equal or greater value. Winners are responsible for any applicable taxes.

8. Odds

This is a contest of skill, not a game of chance. The outcome depends on the quality of entries and the number of eligible entries received.

9. Winner notification

Potential winners are notified by email within 14 days of the end of judging and must respond within 10 days, or an alternate winner may be selected. Potential winners may be required to sign a declaration of eligibility and release.

10. Canadian residents

To be declared a winner, a Canadian resident must first correctly answer, unaided, a time-limited mathematical skill-testing question. Québec residents: any litigation respecting the conduct or organization of a publicity contest may be submitted to the Régie des alcools, des courses et des jeux for a ruling; any litigation respecting the awarding of a prize may be submitted to the Régie only to help the parties reach a settlement.

11. Publicity

Except where prohibited, winners agree that their name, channel or business name, submitted metrics and summary may be used by the Sponsor to announce results and promote future contests, without further compensation.

12. Conduct & disqualification

The Sponsor may disqualify any entrant who tampers with the contest, uses fake or purchased engagement, violates platform policies, submits misleading metrics, or behaves abusively toward other participants.

13. Privacy

Personal information is collected to administer the contest and is handled under our Privacy Policy. It is not sold.

14. Limitation of liability

The Sponsor is not responsible for lost, late or misdirected entries, technical failures, or any injury or loss arising from participation or acceptance of a prize, to the extent permitted by law. The Sponsor may cancel, modify or suspend the contest if fraud, technical failures or other factors impair its integrity, subject to applicable law and any required regulatory approval.

15. Governing law

These rules are governed by the laws of the Province of Québec and the federal laws of Canada applicable therein, without regard to conflict-of-law principles, except where local law requires otherwise.
