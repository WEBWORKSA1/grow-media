How the 30-Day Growth Sprint works

1. Register

Tell us your channel or business and the metric you'll grow. Free, no purchase necessary.

2. Get the start kit

Registrants receive the start date, the sprint playbook and a tracking sheet by email.

3. Sprint for 30 days

Use the guides and tools, share weekly check-ins, and learn alongside other participants.

4. Submit results

Submit before-and-after metrics with screenshots plus a short write-up of what worked.

5. Judging & awards

Growth rate (40%), quality and integrity (30%), learning write-up (30%). Winners are announced in the Weekly Growth Brief.
